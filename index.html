About

I study how environmental change reshapes biodiversity.

My research focuses on biodiversity responses to agricultural land use and atmospheric nitrogen deposition. I work with large-scale biodiversity datasets and statistical models to understand global patterns, and to identify practical ways that natural and semi-natural habitats can support biodiversity in human-modified landscapes.

I completed a year-long research visit with the Global Biodiversity Change group at University College London during my PhD at China Agricultural University.

Research

01

Global biodiversity change

Understanding how land use and other human pressures alter species richness and abundance across regions and taxa.

02

Agricultural landscapes

Comparing biodiversity outcomes among crop types and investigating the value of crop diversification and perennial systems.

03

Nitrogen deposition

Quantifying impacts on arthropod communities and testing whether natural and semi-natural habitats buffer biodiversity loss.
